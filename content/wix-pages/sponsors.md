## JOIN US

It is not easy to prepare and organize a European Championship. Without you, the sponsors, we would never have been able to do that. 
Your participation may not double your profits, but you will support a damn good event.

There’s tons of details and logistics that go into successfully planning and executing this kind of event, so all extra hands are appreciated. You will help motivate young people to play sports, the right values and, last but not least, we believe that thanks to your involvement you will also meet a lot of interesting and inspiring people.

Write to us with details about your possibilities, and someone from our team will be in touch with you ASAP.
