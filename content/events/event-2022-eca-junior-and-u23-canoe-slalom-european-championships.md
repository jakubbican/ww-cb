**Kdy:** 09. 8. 2022 7:00 – 14. 8. 2022 19:00

**Kde:** Vodácký areál České Budějovice, České Vrbné 1995, České Budějovice 2, 370 11 České Budějovice, Česko

The European Junior and U23 Canoe Slalom European Championships will be held in ultimate water-sports area nearby south-bohemian town of České Budějovice. Come and enjoy the features of recently restored white-water course and area as well as the center and surroundings of historical town.
